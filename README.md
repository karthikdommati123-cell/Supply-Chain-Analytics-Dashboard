🚚 Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

📌 Project Overview: 

The Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using Power BI to analyze end-to-end supply chain operations. The dashboard transforms operational data into actionable business insights, enabling organizations to monitor performance, optimize operations, and support strategic decision-making.

The project provides a comprehensive view of inventory, production, logistics, supplier performance, and product quality through interactive dashboards and key performance indicators (KPIs).

🎯 Business Problem: 

Supply chain operations involve multiple interconnected processes, including production, inventory management, logistics, supplier management, and quality control. Monitoring these functions independently often leads to limited visibility, delayed decision-making, and operational inefficiencies.

Some common business challenges include:

High manufacturing and logistics costs.
Inventory shortages or excess stock.
Delayed production and deliveries.
Variations in supplier performance.
Product quality issues due to higher defect rates.
Difficulty identifying operational bottlenecks.

This dashboard addresses these challenges by providing a centralized analytical platform that supports data-driven business decisions.

🎯 Project Objectives: 
Monitor overall supply chain performance.
Analyze inventory availability and production efficiency.
Evaluate logistics and distribution performance.
Assess supplier performance and manufacturing efficiency.
Monitor product quality using defect rates and inspection results.
Support strategic business decisions through interactive dashboards.

📈 Key Business Insights : 
Skincare products generate the highest revenue and production volume.
Supplier 1 contributes the highest revenue, while Supplier 2 has the highest production volume.
Road transportation incurs the highest transportation cost.
Certain delivery routes and locations experience higher shipping times and logistics costs.
Haircare products have the highest defect rate among all product categories.
Supplier performance varies significantly in manufacturing cost, production volume, and lead time.
💡 Business Recommendations ; 
Increase focus on high-performing product categories to maximize revenue.
Optimize inventory planning based on production and sales trends.
Improve logistics efficiency by reducing shipping costs and delivery times.
Strengthen supplier performance monitoring to improve manufacturing efficiency.
Enhance quality control for products with higher defect rates.
Continuously monitor operational KPIs to support strategic business decisions.
